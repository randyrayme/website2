Meet Randy

More than a transaction.

I'm Randy Ray, a South Jersey REALTOR® with Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Fox & Roach. I served in the United States Marine Corps, and through my background in mortgages, extensive credit knowledge, hands-on real estate experience, opening and managing several businesses, I've learned to approach challenges with perspective, practical experience, and a willingness to find solutions.

Whether you're buying, selling, or investing in real estate, I understand that these can be some of the biggest financial decisions you'll make in your lifetime. My goal is to help you understand your options, navigate the challenges, and move forward with confidence.

A lesson I carry from the Marines is to adapt and overcome. My own philosophy goes hand in hand with it: When we get to the bridge, we'll figure out how to cross it together. Whether you're buying, selling, or exploring your next chapter, we'll take the next steps together—starting with a conversation.

I help people make moves they've been thinking about for years.
